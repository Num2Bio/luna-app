What are TACO Points?

Tradable Access to Curator Offerings

TACO points are Luna's dedicated community reward currency! When you donate research vials or menstrual stem cell samples, our clinical lab (Num2Bio) credits your curator account. You can redeem them in the Luna Shop for organic pads, gentle VWash, reusable menstrual cups, and personal care essentials.
